\section{Conclusion}
On the clinical data, kNN and Random Forest predicted CKD best and could not be separated; we would prefer Random Forest for screening because it missed slightly fewer CKD patients in cross-validation. All four models use haemoglobin and specific gravity, and no final model uses more than half of the features; for kNN, removing features helped more than tuning. Hierarchical clustering could recover the two diagnosis groups without the labels, but the result changed a lot with small changes to the features. On full ultrasound images no CNN reached the accuracy of always predicting the majority class; cropping to the annotated boxes gave 88--90\%, but this may reflect the crop more than the pathology. None of this is validated on external data.
